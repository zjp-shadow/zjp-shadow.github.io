# [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12153) · [Mobjaverse Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/duckduckplz/Mobjaverse)

TopoCap extracts motion from monocular video and retargets it onto characters with arbitrary, unseen skeletal topologies, from bipeds to hexapods and inanimate objects, without test-time optimization. We also introduce Mobjaverse, a dataset with more than 5,000 unique skeletal topologies and 2 million frames.
