# [Project Page](/works/SkinTokens/) · [Code](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/SkinTokens) · [Model](https://huggingface.co/VAST-AI/SkinTokens)

SkinTokens is a learned, compact and discrete representation of skinning weights. Built on it, TokenRig generates skeletons and skinning as one autoregressive sequence, improving skinning accuracy by 98%–133% over prior state of the art.
